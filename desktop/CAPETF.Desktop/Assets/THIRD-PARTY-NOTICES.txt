CAPETF Desktop includes these locally packaged third-party components:

- TradingView Lightweight Charts 5.2.0, licensed under Apache-2.0. See
  lightweight-charts.LICENSE.txt and lightweight-charts.NOTICE.txt. The chart
  keeps the TradingView attribution logo and link enabled.
- Lucide 1.27.0, licensed under ISC with listed Feather-derived icons under
  MIT. See lucide.LICENSE.txt.

CAPETF's synthetic-drawings.js was implemented independently using the public
Lightweight Charts v5 primitive APIs. It was not copied from deepentropy or
another third-party drawing implementation.
